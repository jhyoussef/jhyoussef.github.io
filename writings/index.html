GitHub GitVent

I began working on this site in August 2025, and always thought it was taxing. I had it set up to work with in VScode, synced to GitHub. I made my edits, then waited patiently for the deploy workflow to run, checked it out, made more edits. Notice anything? I completely forgot and/or did not know in this usecase one could run local builds for testing site features. My head aches at the thought of all the time wasted for the 5 min sync and deploy cycles, cycles that put me off editing the site and left it in development...
