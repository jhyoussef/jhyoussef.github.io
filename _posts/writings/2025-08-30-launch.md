# Welcome!

Hi! AS I've said in the "about me" post, my name is Julia, a semi-recent graduate from the University of Chicago. I was a geoscience major interested in paleontology, planetary science, and earth history - I also spent time on art and an amateur interest in religious history. 

This site exists for two primary reasons: for one, to document my academic projects, but also, to host my writing and science-related artwork. By recording about academic topics outside the context of papers or reports, I hope to deepen my understanding of my areas of interest -- and by hosting a website, I hope to gather materials into consolidated pages not just for my own use, but also for my fellows researchers. In my writing, I plan to discuss recent publications, artwork, current events, and my own experiences as a first-gen woman in academia. Inspired by Astrobites & 7DaysofScience, that may mean anything from musings on the way we represent evolution, to entries on UChicago specifically, to responses to pop-science essays. Generally, grappling with the role paleontology has in our daily lives, society, and my own academic trajectory. If you're interested in bigger questions – how we define evolutionary "success," where we fit on the tree of life, and how we feel about those relationships – that will likely be here, too.

Oh - and occasionally I'll air my grievances about obscure papers or fawn over the newest discoveries on niche clades (with a special fondness for _Nyctosauridae_ and _Anurognathidae_, both pterosaurs, and some mammalian lineages).
