I have read this work, and much of the subsequent Ender-based series (the *Speaker* series) once every few years since middle school. Every year I seem to gain new insight -- into myself, into empathy, into community. I can't finish the series, anymore. Card's work always betrays him: eventually, the insightful and so-very-humanist themes seem to degrade, and multidimensional characters become husks of themselves performing a Mormon's gender roles. I wish ... I could remember how I felt about these works before I learned of Card's positions. Before I finished the series; before the publication of the last novel, *The Last Shadow* published 25 years after the penultimate *Children of the Mind*, by an author who seems to have forgetten the contents of his own series. 

Ender's Game, and Speaker for the Dead, paradoxically, remain some of my favorite books ever written.
